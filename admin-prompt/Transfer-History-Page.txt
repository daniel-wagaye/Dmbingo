Transfer History Page (Super Admin Read-Only)

Title: Transfer History (Read only)
Top actions
 - Search (server-side): by sender/receiver phone or telegram_id.
 - Filters: Date range (from—to), Wallet (withdrawal / non_withdrawal).
 - Pagination: server-side (default 200 rows/page).
 - Export: CSV (date range + filters) — optional.

Table columns
ID | From (telegram_id / phone masked) | To (telegram_id / phone masked) | Wallet | Amount | Commission | Total | Created At

 - Read-only: no inline actions. Clicking a row opens a details drawer (read-only) showing transfer_history raw fields and the created_at.
